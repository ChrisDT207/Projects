🌡️ Climate Change

Climate change refers to long-term shifts in global temperatures and weather patterns. While climate variations are natural, human activities have been the main driver since the 1800s.

🏭 Causes of Climate Change

🔥 Fossil Fuels

Burning coal, oil, and gas releases CO₂ into the atmosphere

🌳 Deforestation

Cutting down forests reduces Earth's ability to absorb CO₂

🏭 Industry

Manufacturing and cement production release greenhouse gases

🚗 Transportation

Cars, planes, and ships burn fuel and emit carbon dioxide

🌊 Effects of Climate Change

Rising Sea Levels

Melting ice caps cause ocean levels to rise, threatening coastal cities

Extreme Weather

More frequent hurricanes, droughts, floods, and heatwaves

Food Security

Changing weather patterns affect crop growth and food production

Wildlife Impact

Animals lose habitats and struggle to adapt to temperature changes
