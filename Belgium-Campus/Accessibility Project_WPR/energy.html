⚡ Renewable Energy

Renewable energy comes from natural sources that replenish themselves. Unlike fossil fuels, they don't run out and produce little to no pollution.

🔋 Types of Clean Energy

☀️ Solar Power

Converts sunlight into electricity using solar panels

Fastest growing energy source worldwide

💨 Wind Power

Uses wind turbines to generate clean electricity

Can power millions of homes

💧 Hydroelectric

Generates power from flowing or falling water

Oldest renewable energy source

🌍 Geothermal

Harnesses heat from inside the Earth

Available 24/7 regardless of weather

📈 Energy Benefits

🌱 Clean Air

No harmful emissions or air pollution

💰 Cost Savings

Lower electricity bills over time

👷 Jobs

Creates employment in green industries

🔄 Sustainable

Never runs out unlike fossil fuels
