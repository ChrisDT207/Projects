🌟 Take Action for the Planet

Everyone can make a difference! Small changes in daily habits add up to create big positive impacts for our environment.

🏠 At Home

💡 Save Energy

Use LED bulbs and turn off lights when leaving rooms

💧 Conserve Water

Take shorter showers and fix leaky faucets

♻️ Recycle

Sort waste and recycle paper, plastic, and glass

🌡️ Adjust Thermostat

Lower heating and raise cooling by a few degrees

🚶 Transportation

🚶‍♀️ Walk

Great for short distances and your health

🚲 Bike

Fast, fun, and zero emissions

🚌 Public Transit

Buses and trains reduce traffic and pollution

🚗 Carpool

Share rides to reduce fuel use
