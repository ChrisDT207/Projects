🦋 Wildlife Conservation

Conservation protects animals, plants, and their habitats. Biodiversity is essential for healthy ecosystems and human survival.

🌿 Why Conservation Matters

🐝 Pollination

Bees and butterflies help plants reproduce and grow our food

💊 Medicine

Many life-saving drugs come from plants and animals

🌊 Clean Water

Forests and wetlands filter and purify our water supply

🌡️ Climate Control

Trees absorb CO₂ and help regulate Earth's temperature

🏞️ Protected Areas

🏔️ National Parks

Large areas protected for wildlife and recreation

🌊 Marine Reserves

Ocean areas where fishing and development are limited

🌳 Forest Reserves

Protected woodlands that preserve tree species

🦆 Wildlife Refuges

Safe spaces for endangered animals to live and breed

⚠️ Threats to Wildlife

Habitat Loss

Development destroys where animals live

Pollution

Chemicals harm wildlife and ecosystems

Climate Change

Temperature changes affect animal behavior

Overhunting

Taking too many animals from the wild
